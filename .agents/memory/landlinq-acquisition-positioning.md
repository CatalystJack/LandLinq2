LandLinq plugs into your inbox and runs your acquisitions outreach on autopilot. It sends personalized drip campaigns to every broker in the states you pick, and to anyone else you want to stay top of mind with: GCs, engineers, architects. When deals come back in, it screens them right away against your criteria, like county, minimum acreage and minimum rents, so your team only spends time on the ones that fit. You get a bigger acquisitions team without the headcount.

**How to apply:** Use it for the Investment Company and acquisitions audience, distinct from broker-facing messaging.
