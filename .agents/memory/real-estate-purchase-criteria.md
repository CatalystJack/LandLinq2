---
name: Real-estate purchase criteria
description: Product requirement for separate land and existing-property acquisition screens in real-estate company profiles.
---

Real-estate companies need to configure both land-purchase criteria and existing-property criteria independently. Land criteria include acreage and zoning unit capacity; existing-property criteria include vintage and unit count.

**Why:** the user requested both purchase strategies and named vintage and unit count as existing-property filters.

**How to apply:** keep these thresholds separate from comparable-search filters and from multifamily development product-type rent assumptions. Treat missing evidence as review rather than silently passing or rejecting when a configured threshold depends on that evidence.
