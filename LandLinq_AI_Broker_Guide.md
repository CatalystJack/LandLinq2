### How Our AI Evaluates Properties

LandLinq's AI considers dozens of factors when analyzing your deals:

**Location Intelligence:**
- Proximity to target demographics (especially 55+ communities for Active Adult)
- School districts and employment centers
- Infrastructure and utility availability
- Market growth trends and development pipeline

**Financial Analysis:**
- Price per acre vs. market comparables
- Development cost projections
- Revenue potential based on product type
- ROI calculations and cap rate analysis

**Regulatory Assessment:**
- Zoning compatibility with intended use
- Entitlement requirements and timeline
- Environmental constraints
- Municipal approval likelihood

**Risk Evaluation:**
- Market saturation analysis
- Construction feasibility
- Exit strategy viability
- Competitive landscape assessment
