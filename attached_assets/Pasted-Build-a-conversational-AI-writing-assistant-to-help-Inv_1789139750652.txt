Build a conversational AI writing assistant to help Investment Companies draft 
and refine their outreach/drip campaign emails, integrated directly into the 
campaign step editor in client/src/pages/developer-outreach.tsx (find the exact 
email body editor component for a campaign step — likely a Textarea or rich-text 
editor where campaign email content is composed — and add this assistant 
alongside it).

TASK 1 — Personalized context for the AI
When generating or refining email copy, give the AI access to this specific 
company's own data: their company name and branding, their acquisition criteria 
(target counties/states, rent thresholds, product types), and optionally which 
contact tag this campaign is targeting (e.g. "broker" vs "architect" — tone 
should differ appropriately). Fetch this the same way other developer-scoped 
data is fetched elsewhere (via getDeveloperProfileId), so the AI always writes in 
context of the correct company, never generic or mixed up with another company's 
data.

TASK 2 — Conversational chat interface
Add a chat panel next to (or toggleable alongside) the email body editor — not a 
one-shot generate button, but a real back-and-forth conversation. Support 
requests like: "write an email introducing us to brokers in our target 
counties," "make it shorter," "make it more formal," "add a line about our rent 
threshold." Each response updates a live draft the user can review.

TASK 3 — Explicit "Insert" action, never silent overwrite
The AI's draft should NOT automatically overwrite whatever's currently in the 
email body editor. Show the AI's suggested draft clearly in the chat panel with 
an explicit "Insert into email" or "Replace draft" button — the user decides 
when to actually apply it, protecting any manual edits they may have already 
made.

TASK 4 — Reuse existing AI infrastructure
Use the same AI provider/model already integrated for email parsing 
(aiEmailParser.ts) rather than adding a new AI vendor relationship.

TASK 5 — Keep conversation history per campaign step
Persist the chat conversation for a given campaign step (at least for the current 
editing session, ideally saved so they can return to refine it later) so context 
isn't lost if they navigate away and come back.

Verify: confirm a test company can ask for a draft referencing their own real 
target counties and rent threshold, confirm iterative refinement requests 
("make it shorter") work correctly on the existing draft rather than starting 
over, and confirm nothing is inserted into the actual email until the user 
explicitly clicks to apply it.
