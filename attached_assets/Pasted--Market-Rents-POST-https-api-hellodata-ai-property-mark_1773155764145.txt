# Market Rents

POST https://api.hellodata.ai/property/market_rents
Content-Type: application/json

Get market rents for active units among a subject property given its details. We use all property and unit details (characteristics, amenities, location, etc.)
to find similar units in the market that have been rented recently. We then use the rent prices of these units to estimate the unit pricing.

This is an alias for the pricing endpoint with a more descriptive name.

Reference: https://docs.hellodata.ai/api-reference/api-reference/property/market-rents
