TASK 2 — Fix the address/coordinate handling to detect conflicts, not silently overwrite
In server/automatedDealEmailPipeline.ts (~line 156), the current logic silently

coordinates are present. Change this: when BOTH a stated address/county-state AND

disagreed. Only use the reverse-geocoded value automatically when there was no 
stated county/state to compare against in the first place (the current coordinate- 
only fallback case, which is fine as-is).
