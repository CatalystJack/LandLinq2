(@apexresi.com), but that migration was only applied to a small number of newer 
routes. 220 occurrences of the old @catalystcp.com check remain across 
server/routes.ts, alongside the 3 correctly-migrated @apexresi.com checks. This 
means most of the app's internal admin/analyst permission checks are currently 
STALE and likely locking the real team out of their own tools.

"includes('@catalyst'" (220 total). For each one, categorize it as:

      should become userEmail.endsWith('@apexresi.com')

      text anywhere would incorrectly pass)
Report back a categorized list before making changes, so we can confirm the (b) 
cases are correctly identified — do not guess if a case is ambiguous, list it 
separately for a decision.
