# Access 3DHP Data Products

By [3D Hydrography Program](https://www.usgs.gov/3d-hydrography-program)

3D Hydrography Program data and legacy National Hydrography data products are available for download and as web-based map and data services.

## 3D Hydrography Program Data Products

3DHP products are available in two forms – web services and a downloadable product.  New Elevation-Derived Hydrography ( [EDH](https://www.usgs.gov/ngp-standards-and-specifications/elevation-derived-hydrography-specifications)) data and attributes will be added to the services quarterly, and the downloadable product will be updated early each Fiscal Year.  The annual downloadable products will remain available for reference with Digital Object Identifiers.

## Known 3DHP Data Issues

- Flow Network Derivatives were not calculated for the Big Darby Creek in Ohio (Workunit ID 300290) in the July 2026 release.  This issue will be corrected in the October release.

## Legacy National Hydrography Products

In 2024, the USGS shifted from maintaining the National Hydrography Dataset (NHD), the Watershed Boundary Dataset (WBD), and the NHDPlus High-Resolution to production of 3D Hydrography Program products.  While these legacy products are no longer updated and maintained, they are provided here as both downloadable products and as services for reference purposes.  More information about the legacy datasets is available on the [About National Hydrography Products webpage.](https://www.usgs.gov/national-hydrography/about-national-hydrography-products)
